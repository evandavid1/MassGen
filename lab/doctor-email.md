Following my recent lipid panel results, I've done significant research on the best approach for managing my cholesterol given my suspected familial hypercholesterolemia. The clinical evidence strongly supports **Crestor 10mg + Zetia 10mg over increasing to Crestor 20mg**, and I'm requesting this prescription change.

**The case for combination therapy:**

Doubling a statin dose only provides an incremental 6-8% additional LDL reduction, while adding ezetimibe provides 15-24% additional reduction through a completely different mechanism. The combination also carries a better safety profile — this isn't a close call.

**Key findings from the research:**

- **Same LDL reduction, fewer side effects:** A 2025 head-to-head study found rosuvastatin 10mg + ezetimibe matched rosuvastatin 20mg for LDL reduction, with fewer muscle symptoms and liver enzyme elevations. For a 51-year-old on long-term therapy, this matters.

- **19% mortality reduction:** A 2024 meta-analysis of 108,373 patients showed statin + ezetimibe reduced all-cause mortality by 19%, cardiovascular events by 18%, and stroke by 17% — with no increase in adverse events.

- **57% LDL reduction in FH patients:** Combination therapy achieved 57% LDL reduction in familial hypercholesterolemia patients regardless of mutation type. This should return me to the 93 mg/dL I achieved in July 2024.

- **Statin muscle risk is dose-dependent:** The evidence is clear that higher statin doses increase myopathy risk. The smarter approach is maximizing efficacy at a tolerable dose by adding ezetimibe.

- **This combination is underutilized, not unproven:** Only 3% of statin users are prescribed ezetimibe despite 66% of CV patients failing to reach LDL goals on statin alone. The evidence supports broader use — the guidelines already recommend it.

**What I'm requesting:**

1. **Crestor 10mg + Zetia 10mg** prescription (or Rosuzet if preferred)

3. **Follow-up labs in 8 weeks** to confirm response
